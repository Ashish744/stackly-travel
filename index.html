Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect only what you enter on this website: destination searches, travel dates, guest and room counts, your wishlist and the email address you use to subscribe to our newsletter.

2. How we use it

Your details are used to show relevant hotels, estimate booking prices, remember your favourites and send travel deals you have asked for. We never sell your personal data.

3. Local storage

Your wishlist and newsletter subscription are saved in your browser's LocalStorage on your own device. You can clear them any time from your browser settings.

4. Cookies & third parties

This page loads fonts and animation scripts from third-party providers (Google Fonts and unpkg), which may receive standard technical data such as your IP address. Site images are stored locally.

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no online method is completely secure.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or unsubscribe from emails at any time by contacting us.
